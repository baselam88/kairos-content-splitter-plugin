# One Idea, Many Pieces

Splits one anchor asset into 4-5 native derivatives — 60s short script, text post (hook in first 210 chars), reel script, email version — each rewritten for its surface.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

Never cross-posts raw. Respects one best piece per day on LinkedIn-class surfaces; external links stay out of post bodies.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the One Idea, Many Pieces to help me: splits one anchor asset into 4-5 native derivatives — 60s short script, text post (hook in first 210 chars), reel script, email version — each rewritten for its surface — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.
